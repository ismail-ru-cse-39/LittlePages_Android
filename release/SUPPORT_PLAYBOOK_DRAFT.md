# LittlePages — customer support playbook draft

**Status:** internal draft. Assign a support owner, service hours, response targets, contact channels, privacy/legal language, and escalation contacts before launch.

## Intake rules

- Ask for the parent's account email or order reference only when needed. Never request a child's full name, school, photo, password, verification code, payment card number, or bKash PIN.
- Ask for app version, Android version/device model, selected app language, approximate time, and a concise description of what happened.
- For reading issues, ask for book ID/title and page number. Ask whether the issue is online or with a previously authorized offline copy.
- For a payment issue, collect the provider's transaction/reference ID and order ID; do not collect card credentials or wallet PINs.
- Tell the parent not to attach a child's face or identifying information to a public community post or support request.

## First response checklist

1. Acknowledge the reported issue and confirm the affected feature.
2. Check the service status and current incident notices before asking the parent to repeat steps.
3. Offer the relevant in-app recovery action: retry book access, refresh offline access, retry order status, or contact support from Parent Profile.
4. Do not promise a purchase, refund, entitlement, or delivery change until the trusted order service confirms it.
5. Record the case reference, time, affected service, app version, and next update owner.

## Escalation guide

| Priority | Example | Escalation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P0 — urgent | Suspected child-safety exposure, account takeover, or broad data exposure | Immediately notify the designated safety/security on-call; preserve the incident reference and follow the approved incident plan. |
| P1 — service outage | Many families cannot sign in, open books, or check order status | Notify the service owner; link the alert/incident and provide a status-update time. |
| P2 — account/order case | One family's access, payment, refund, or delivery issue | Route to the authorized account or commerce team; verify server-side state before changing it. |
| P3 — product feedback | Content correction, usability issue, or feature request | Log a product ticket without unnecessary family or child data. |

Priority targets, service hours, contact addresses, refund authority, and regional child-safety escalation obligations require owner and legal approval; this table is not an operational SLA.

## Suggested support macros

### Offline book expired

Ask the parent to connect to the internet and refresh the book's access. Do not suggest copying the book file or changing device time. If renewal still fails, collect the book title, app version, and case reference.

### Payment pending

Ask the parent to avoid paying again while the transaction is pending. Collect the order ID and provider transaction reference, then check the verified order state. Never request card details, one-time codes, or wallet PINs.

### Narration or highlighting issue

Ask for the book title, page, selected voice/language, Android version, and whether narration is ready on another page. Explain that voice availability can depend on Android's installed speech engine. Escalate repeatable timing/content mismatches to the book-content team.
